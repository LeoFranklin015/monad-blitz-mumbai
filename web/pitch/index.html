The problem

Stale price. Sudden gap. Everyone liquidated.

The products

Upside to ride the gain. Floor to survive the gap.

UPSIDE · call
The right to buy at the strike. Leverage with a known worst case.
Stock up →profit rises with the stock, no cap
Stock down →walk away, you only lose the premium
FLOOR · put
The right to sell at the strike. A hard floor under your stock.
Stock up →keep it, let the floor expire
Gap down →sell at the strike, any time — even Saturday
The difference

Same 12% gap. Perp → $0. Option → safe.
